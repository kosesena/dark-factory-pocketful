## Ambiguities and extra behavior

- A caller-omitted split is explicitly permitted, but “shares covers every participant including the caller” could be read otherwise. Use listed handles only; observed service follows that reading.
- Final reset/import preservation wording cannot mean keeping pre-reset state: reset explicitly replaces everything with its fixture. Interpret preservation against supplied data.
- Zero-share requests are legal, so paying them at zero is the consistent reading even though direct creation requires amount ≥1. Observed service supports it.
- Import accepts an operator ID that names no account. The extra two failed expectations for that case are **advisory**, since the opaque state's treatment of future/nonexistent operator IDs is not fully specified. F4's timestamp/range violations are independently blocking.
- Unknown routes return JSON 404; server source additionally offers 405 method_not_allowed for unmatched methods on known paths. This extra method behavior is not specified and is not a rejection reason.
- Transient internal balance safety, unbounded token lifetime, and independent development provenance remain unverifiable rather than silently accepted.
