## Stage-3 requirement evidence
- **Payment timestamps:** a seeded future `created_at` → 422 with no change. Seeded balances are not replayed. Omitted `created_at` uses reset time.
- **`/me?as_of`:**
  - naive, bare-date, empty, invalid-month and free-text values → 422 (also for `known_at`);
  - the value is echoed exactly, including a `+02:00` offset;
  - inclusive at the payment instant;
  - before the earliest payment it returns the opening balance; after the latest, and in the future, the current balance;
  - the sum across users equals the seeded total at every instant checked, before and after corrections.
- **`/statement`:**
  - only own payments (excludes public payments between others), oldest first, ties by id;
  - half-open window; opening and closing balances; deltas sum to the closing balance;
  - paging keeps `balance_after` and the opening/closing balances; `has_more` correct on the last page and past the end;
  - entries carry `revision`, `effective_at` and `recorded_at`;
  - bad parameters → 422; no token → 401.
- **Snapshots:**
  - a token on the first read; paging a snapshot is frozen across new payments and corrections;
  - `from`, `to` or `known_at` with a snapshot → 422;
  - another user's token, an unknown token, or a token from before reset → 404;
  - unknown parameters are ignored.
- **Corrections:**
  - no key → 400; no token → 401; receiver or third party → 403; unknown payment → 404;
  - 14 invalid bodies → 422 (a wrong-typed `expected_revision` → 400 is accepted under §5);
  - 201 shape; a decrease debits the receiver and an increase debits the sender;
  - replay → 200 identical, even after newer revisions; key reuse → 409; stale revision → 409;
  - `insufficient_funds` takes precedence over `historical_overdraft`; both failures change nothing;
  - a zero amount reverses the payment and appears as a zero-delta entry;
  - moving the effective time moves the payment across a window and reorders entries by effective time;
  - `/activity` still shows the original payment once; opening balances are unchanged;
  - `recorded_at` strictly increases;
  - 20 concurrent corrections with the same expected revision → exactly one 201 and 19 × 409.
- **Revisions endpoint:** visible to both parties; a third party gets 404 even for a public payment; no token → 401; revision 1 has `reason ""`.
- **`known_at`:** selects the latest revision recorded at or before it, otherwise the payment contributes nothing. Checked exactly at, just before and after recorded times, and in the future.
- **Settlements and captures:**
  - a settlement member's revision 1 has `effective_at` = `recorded_at` = `committed_at`;
  - correcting a member → 422 `linked_payment_immutable`, and the same for a capture;
  - a capture appears once in the statement; a closed authorization has `closed_at`, an open one `closed_at: null`.
- **Historical holds:** before creation `held` is 0; after creation `held` is 2000 and `available` = `total` − 2000; in a future view past the deadline it has expired (`held` 0); holds are not statement entries.
