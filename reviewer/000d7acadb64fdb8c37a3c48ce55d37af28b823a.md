## Finding 1 (accepted limitation, ruling dfc2d281): many genuine legacy statements with many later facts exceed the 5 s per-request limit
- **Requirements:** the run contract ("5s per request"), stage 4 ("must accept exports produced by the same team's stages 1–3, retaining ... snapshots"), and the round-7 handoff ("Every case must be about 2 s and certainly under 5 s ... Look for any further adversarial schedule").
- **Genuine reproduction:** `verification/probes_legacy_migration.py http://localhost:9504 http://localhost:9533 10000 300`, which takes 300 real statements on 495d5d6 and then makes 10000 later payments. The genuine import gives 204 in **5.20 s**, and the tampered import (one snapshot shifted +1) gives 422 in **6.15 s**. Both fail the "within 5 s" checks; the other 355 checks pass.
- **Scaling**, from the 10000-fact E3 ledger (3 users, same-amount corrections plus out-of-window payments), with the legacy snapshots repeated under new tokens. A repeated GET of the same query yields exactly such snapshots.

  | legacy snapshots | genuine | tampered |
  |---|---|---|
  | 20 | 0.81 s | 0.56 s |
  | 50 | 1.23 s | 1.15 s |
  | 100 | 2.42 s | 2.17 s |
  | 200 | 4.27 s | 4.15 s |
  | 250 | **5.44 / 5.29 s** | **5.41 s** |
  | 300 | **6.75 / 6.68 s** | **6.44 s** |
  | 500 | **10.44 / 10.69 s** | **10.78 s** |

  A genuine export with 500 statements and 5000 later payments takes 3.59 s (genuine) and 3.88 s (tampered).
- **Cause, read in the diff:** `_find_moment` still walks every recorded moment once per legacy snapshot, with three Fenwick updates per moment. That is about 20 ms per snapshot at 10000 facts: O(snapshots × moments × log N). The hash screen removed the repeated rebuilds, but not this per-snapshot walk.
- **Smallest reproduction:**
  1. On 495d5d6: reset, log in as u_a, then `GET /statement?limit=1` 300 times.
  2. Make 10000 payments of 1 from a to b.
  3. Export, then time `POST /_test/import` on stage 4 running with 2 CPU / 2 GiB. It takes more than 5 s.
- **For the coordinator:** if a bound on legacy statements per export is ruled acceptable, nothing else blocks. At 10000 later facts, up to about 200 statements stay under 5 s and up to about 100 stay near 2 s.
