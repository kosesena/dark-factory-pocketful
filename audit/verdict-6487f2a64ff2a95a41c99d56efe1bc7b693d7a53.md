## Blocking
**P1: legacy-snapshot import is quadratic and exceeds the request limits.**
- Requirements:
  - Run contract: 5 s per request. The cross-auditor adds the 10 s control-call limit.
  - S4-36: "A stage-4 service must accept exports produced by the same team's stages 1–3, retaining … snapshots."
  - Inherited §10: an invalid state gives 422, not a timeout.
- Evidence: `audit/probes/legacy_migration_probes.py SRC DEST N`. The source is a real 495d5d6 stage-3 service from
  `git archive 495d5d6`. The script resets, ada pays 1, ada takes a statement, N more payments follow, then it exports
  and imports into a clean archive of 6487f2a. Timings on my Mac (faster than the harness's 2 vCPU):

  | N | genuine import | "shift balances +1" tamper |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2500 | 204 in 2.41 s | — |
  | 3000 | 204 in 2.76 s | 422 in 2.97 s |
  | 4000 | 204 in 6.32 s | — |
  | 6000 | 204 in 15.79 s | 422 in 12.97 s |

  The same 6000-payment export with its snapshots removed imports in 0.22 s.
- Cause: `_past_moments` checks every moment against every fact, and the search runs a full `build_statement` per
  candidate moment. Both are O(N²) in the facts after the snapshot.
