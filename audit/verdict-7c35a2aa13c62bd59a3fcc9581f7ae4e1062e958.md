## Interpretations and non-blocking notes
- Payment `created_at` (and so revision 1's effective_at and recorded_at) is stamped to the whole second. Correction
  recorded times carry microseconds.
  - The service uses the truncated instant everywhere, including hold events, so its views agree with each other.
  - Side effect: a query with `as_of` or `known_at` a fraction of a second before a payment counts that payment
    whenever both fall in the same second.
  - RFC 3339 permits second precision, so I read this as allowed and not blocking. Microsecond stamps would remove it.
